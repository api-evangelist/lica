# Lica

> Lica is an AI research company building a substrate to train and evaluate AI models
> as capable design partners. Its research targets spatial reasoning and composition,
> layout generation and editing, vector-graphics (SVG) understanding, personalization
> and brand identity, and animation generation. Lica publishes the LICA dataset of
> layered graphic-design compositions and GDB (Graphic Design Benchmark), a
> real-world benchmark scoring frontier models on designer workflows. Backed by
> Accel, South Park Commons and Village Global. Lica does not currently publish a
> public API, developer portal, or API reference; api.lica.world exists but requires
> authentication.

## Open source
- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/lica-world): Lica's public repositories
- [DesignMCP](https://github.com/lica-world/DesignMCP): First-party MCP server for AI
  design/multimedia tooling (Streamable HTTP, self-hosted via Docker Compose, port 8000)
- [GDB](https://github.com/lica-world/GDB): GraphicDesignBench — real-world benchmark for
  evaluating AI on graphic-design tasks
- [lica-dataset](https://github.com/lica-world/lica-dataset): Graphic-design layouts with
  component-level specs and annotations
- [harbor](https://github.com/lica-world/harbor): Framework for running agent evaluations
  and building RL environments
